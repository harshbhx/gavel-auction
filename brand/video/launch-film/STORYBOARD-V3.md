# GAVEL launch film v3: storyboard

A complete redesign of the launch film after Harsh rejected v2 ("not at all like Stripe or Apple"; too many framed
screen recordings, too little real motion). Version 3 is **fully motion graphics**: every product surface (bidder
phone, auctioneer console, big screen, hold card, receipt, ledger, judge panel) is rebuilt as a native React
component inside Remotion from the app's own copy (`packages/web/src/copy/en.ts`) and tokens
(`packages/web/src/design/tokens.css`), so every element can move: springs, staggered reveals, shared-element
transitions between scenes, mask wipes, rolling numbers, a bid ladder that climbs, hold chips that stack and release,
a waveform that reacts to words, and a 3D camera (perspective, rotateX/Y, dolly, parallax, depth blur, whip pans).
No framed screen recording anywhere. 1920x1080, 60 fps, narration captions burned in, 2:44.5 (164.5 s), in two
files: a silent cut and a voice-over cut with the same picture.
Built by Harsh Bhanushali and Dhairya Patel, credited in the hook, the title and the outro.

## The one idea

"Every bid is a hold. The hammer is the payment." Said in the hook (as a number), the title (as type), the mechanism
(as a diagram), the hammer (as a strike) and the settle (as a receipt). Everything else is the room in motion.

## Rhythm: 120 bpm grid

- Beat 0.5 s, bar 2 s. Every scene is a whole number of beats; every cut lands on a beat; stamps, strikes, the
  hammer release and the first word of a caption land on a beat (the composer prints the real times to
  `timeline.json`).
- Hard cuts only between scenes, except three shared-element transitions (join code phone -> big screen, bid chip
  phone -> ladder, winning hold tile hammer -> settle) and two mask wipes (title -> problem, settle -> receipt).
- No static hold longer than 1 s anywhere: the camera always drifts (1.02 to 1.08 over a scene), the glows breathe,
  the grain moves. Verified by the frame-difference check in `report.txt` (see "Verification").
- Each scene runs at least three layers at once: camera, a type layer and a product component, plus glows and grain.

## Motion language (every scene obeys it)

| Element    | Spec                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Entrances  | Line masks (overflow hidden) rising 110 % -> 0, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, 0.6 to 0.9 s, stagger 70 to 90 ms; letter-spacing tightens 0.06 em -> 0 as a line lands (Stripe headline settle). |
| UI physics | Remotion `spring()` (damping 14 to 18, stiffness 120 to 180) for chips, ladder rows, buttons, stamps; springs only on UI, never on type or camera.                                                 |
| Camera     | One perspective stage (`perspective: 1800px`); moves are dolly (scale 1.02 to 1.12), rotateX/Y up to 14 deg, whip pans 0.35 s with 6 px motion blur, `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)`.             |
| Depth      | Three parallax layers (glow field far, product plane mid, type near), depth-of-field blur 0 to 6 px on far layers while the camera is on the near layer.                                           |
| Numbers    | Odometer digit columns (each digit rolls on its own), tabular figures, 0.5 s per roll.                                                                                                             |
| Light      | A 40 deg light sweep (cream at 14 % alpha) crosses a plane once per scene; glow fields drift at 0.02 of the frame per second; grain 5 % moving every frame.                                        |
| Palette    | Ink `#1d1712`, paper `#efe6d3`, orange `#ff6a1f`; deep ink stage `#120e0b` -> `#1d1712` gradients with orange and cream glows; paper scenes `#efe6d3` -> `#f8f2e4`. No blue, navy or grey UI.      |
| Type       | Big Shoulders Display 120 to 320 px for display lines (one or two words a line, one orange word at most), Public Sans for UI copy, JetBrains Mono for eyebrows, labels and the sandbox footer.     |
| Exits      | `cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)` 0.3 s, or the next scene's mask wipe.                                                                                                                              |

## Final cut: narration, captions, no addresses (10 Nov)

- **Narration.** One take of the script in `graphics/motion-film-v3/voiceover.mjs` (ElevenLabs, voice "Ratchet v14
  Narrator", 131 s), cut at its sentence pauses into 39 clips and placed on the film timeline so each line lands on
  its picture: "held" on the HELD stamp, "going once, going twice, sold" on the hammer ring and the strike, "the
  chain breaks" on the edited ledger row. The track is `graphics/motion-film-v3/audio/voiceover.mp3`. No music.
- **Captions.** The same 55 lines are burned into the picture (`src/captions.tsx`, `src/captions.json`): one line,
  bottom centre, Public Sans 600 at 50 px on an ink plate, timed from the take's word timings. Every scene draws
  inside a picture area (87 % of the frame, top) so the caption band never covers the product. The SRT is the same cues.
- **No addresses on screen.** The speed, judge and outro scenes name the try page, the docs and the source without a
  URL; the links live in the video description and the Devpost page.
- **Timing changes for the narration.** Proof runs 12.5 s (the card the narration is on lights up), open items 9 s,
  the hammer press starts at 3.0 s, the winner's capture at 3.2 s of the settle scene.
- **Fixes from the stills review.** The mechanism diagram fits the frame (the $500 label was clipped), the bid chips
  no longer collide, the proof camera no longer pans the headline out of frame, the speed headline reads on two lines,
  odometer digits are centred in their columns, the honesty line moved to the top right, grain steps every second frame.

## Production

- `graphics/motion-film-v3/` is a self-contained Remotion project (React components, no recording input). `compose.mjs`
  links the app fonts and brand SVGs into `public/`, renders `Film` with `npx remotion render`, writes the share copy,
  poster, SRT, `timeline.json`, 4 fps contact sheets, the frame-difference check and `report.txt`.
- `SCENES=hook,title,mechanism` renders a subset (the style test); `FPS=30` and `SCALE=0.6667` give a 720p30 pass.
- Workflow: `.github/workflows/film-v3-render.yml`: four matrix jobs render a quarter of the frames each
  (`SHARD=i/4`), the finish job stitches them (`CHUNKS=<dir>`), caps the size, adds the narration to a copy and
  writes the share copies. On the film branch a push that bumps `graphics/motion-film-v3/RENDER` starts it; once the
  workflow is on the default branch it is a manual dispatch. `film-v3-stills.yml` renders five stills per scene.

## Verification

- Frame difference: the composer samples the render at 4 fps to 96x54 grey, computes the mean absolute difference of
  consecutive samples and lists every run of 4 or more samples under 0.6 % as a static hold (the target is zero).
- Contact sheets at 4 fps are reviewed for weak scenes before the final commit.
- Caption durations under 2.0 s are listed as warnings.
