The web app is at [gavel.dualmindlab.tech](https://gavel.dualmindlab.tech) and the API at [api.gavel.dualmindlab.tech](https://api.gavel.dualmindlab.tech), both in sandbox mode with a persistent disk. You need no login and no key. A <Term id="judge-room">judge room</Term> lasts about 30 minutes, so do the five actions in one sitting.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open /try

Open [gavel.dualmindlab.tech/try](https://gavel.dualmindlab.tech/try) and press "Start a demo auction". If judge rooms are off or the host is busy, the page says so and offers the [scripted demo](https://gavel.dualmindlab.tech/demo/scripted).

Expected: a room hub with a room code, a QR code and buttons for the big screen, the console and the bidder view.

</Step>
<Step>

### Join as a bidder

On the hub, join as a bidder with any name and a ceiling (by button, room code, or the QR code on a phone). Keep the big screen open in a second window. "Join with the demo" needs no PayPal login; to see a sandbox <Term id="hold">hold</Term> instead, press and hold the approve button for 3 seconds and use a buyer from [Sandbox buyers](/docs/judges/sandbox-buyers).

Expected: you appear in the room with your ceiling, and the hold reads as held.

</Step>
<Step>

### Bid

Place a bid from the bidder view.

Expected: the price on the big screen moves. Hatched means held, nothing is charged yet.

</Step>
<Step>

### Hold the hammer on the console

Open the console from the hub and press and hold the hammer button.

Expected: the lot closes. One winner is <Term id="capture">captured</Term> at the winning price and every other hold is <Term id="void">released</Term> (solid means charged, hollow means released).

</Step>
<Step>

### Read the receipt and the ledger

Open the receipt, then the audit trail, from the room.

Expected: the receipt shows what was charged, and the audit trail is a <Term id="ledger">ledger</Term> chained by hash. You can re-check it in the [ten-minute path](/docs/judges/ten-minute-path).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Then check what is proven

The five actions show the product. These pages show what each claim is backed by.

1. [Gates G0 to G5](/docs/proof/gates): <Term id="gate-g0">G0</Term> to G4 PASS on the real sandbox, <Term id="gate-g5">G5</Term> partial. The table is generated from `docs/GATES.md`, so it cannot differ from the repository.
2. [Voice and the confirm gate](/docs/how-it-works/voice-and-confirm): why every spoken bid is confirmed before it counts.
3. [Money flow](/docs/how-it-works/money-flow) and [Settlement walkthrough](/docs/how-it-works/settlement-walkthrough): one capture, the rest released.
4. [The 30-day simulation](/docs/proof/simulation): eight scenarios, all PASS. They run our code against a model of PayPal on a fake clock, so they do not replace the real sandbox result of gate G3.

## What you have seen

- Real on the PayPal sandbox: gates G0, G1, G2, G3 and G4.
- Partial: G5. Speech accuracy was measured on machine-generated test voice clips, and the confirm prompt protects every spoken bid.
- Not yet proven: a room joined with "Join with the demo" uses test holds with no PayPal call. Real sandbox settlement has run for one lot of three holds; a whole auction settled through the hosted server on the real sandbox is a next step.
