## The idea in one paragraph

When the <Term id="hammer">hammer</Term> falls, GAVEL has one winning price and several <Term id="hold">holds</Term>. It charges the winner the winning price, releases the part of the winner's hold that was not used, and releases every other hold. The [settlement saga](/docs/how-it-works/settlement-saga) does this as a journaled state machine, so a crash in the middle cannot charge twice or forget a hold. This page walks one concrete lot through it, in whole cents (money in GAVEL is always integer cents).

## The worked example

Three buyers each hold **200.00 (20000 cents)**. The <Term id="engine">engine</Term> ranks the <Term id="bid">bids</Term> and prices each candidate as if every earlier candidate were out:

| Candidate | Hold ceiling | Price at the hammer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A (winner) | 20000 | 12500 |
| B (runner-up) | 20000 | 11000 |
| C | 20000 | 10000 |

These are the numbers of the nightly <Term id="sandbox">sandbox</Term> spec R7. Use the buttons under the figure to switch between the normal sale and the case where the winner's <Term id="capture">capture</Term> is refused.

## What each step does

<Steps>
<Step>

### Plan

`planFromOutcome` turns the engine's hammer outcome into a plan: the candidates in the engine's order at the engine's prices, plus each bidder's hold. A candidate with no hold, or whose price is above its own hold's ceiling, cannot be charged and is left out, so the next candidate becomes the fallback instead of the whole plan being rejected. The plan is the first thing saved to the <Term id="journal">journal</Term>.

</Step>
<Step>

### Intent, then capture

Before the capture call the <Term id="saga">saga</Term> saves the intent to the journal: capture, the hold, the amount and a request id. The capture is `final_capture` for the candidate's own price, with an invoice id. A retry or a resume sends the **same request id**, so PayPal answers it once. See [Idempotency](/docs/how-it-works/idempotency).

</Step>
<Step>

### Remainder

After CAPTURED the saga reads the winner's authorization again. Gate <Term id="gate-g1">G1</Term> observed on the sandbox that after a final capture the authorization reads CAPTURED and the order COMPLETED, and that a later void answers 422 `PREVIOUSLY_CAPTURED`. So by default (decision A) the saga records the remainder as closed by PayPal and sends no <Term id="void">void</Term>. If PayPal ever reports an open remainder, the saga voids it explicitly (decision B), and a void refused because the authorization is already captured, voided or expired counts as released.

</Step>
<Step>

### Void the others

Every hold except the winner's is voided, sorted by bidder id, at most three at a time (`voidConcurrency`), each under its own fixed request id. A void answered "already voided" or "expired" counts as released. A void that keeps failing is retried on later runs; after five resumes (`maxVoidResumes`) the settlement goes to review.

</Step>
<Step>

### Settled

When every hold is charged or released, the journal moves to SETTLED. The <Term id="ledger">ledger</Term> balances: held cents equal charged plus released.

</Step>
</Steps>

## If the winner's capture is refused

Only a **definite** refusal that names this hold (declined, expired, already voided, not found) moves the settlement on. The saga first reads the hold: if a capture already exists it is adopted. If not, it records the failure and charges the next candidate at **that candidate's own planned price**, never at the failed winner's price and never at a runner-up's maximum bid. In the example, B is charged 11000, not 12500 and not 20000.

An **unknown** answer (a timeout or a 5xx) is not a refusal. The settlement stays in CAPTURING and waits; it never moves to the next bidder, because the capture may have happened. After ten minutes it goes to review. See [Retries and unknown outcomes](/docs/how-it-works/retries-and-unknown-outcomes).

## What is verified, and what is not

<Charged title="Sandbox evidence for the sale path">

Nightly sandbox spec R7 (workflows `nightly-sandbox`, runs of 6 and 7 Nov 2026 on `main`) settled a lot of three holds with the real saga and the real gateway: winner captured 12500 cents, saga SETTLED, journal against PayPal 0 differences. Those orders are PayPal-account orders.

</Charged>

<NotVerified title="Inferred, simulated and fake-only parts">

- That the winner's remainder is released is **inferred** from the CAPTURED reading and the 422 on a later void (gate G1 decision A). The authorization keeps its original amount, and no run read a buyer balance.
- The runner-up fallback and the void retries ran against the **fake PayPal**, not the sandbox.
- Capture of a guest card authorization was never run on the sandbox.

</NotVerified>
