## In plain words

PayPal does not let a <Term id="hold">hold</Term> stay capturable forever. An <Term id="authorization">authorization</Term> has an **<Term id="honor-period">honor period</Term>**: PayPal promises to honor a <Term id="capture">capture</Term> for a limited time after the hold, and may decline later ones. The authorization is also **valid** for a longer window. A **<Term id="reauthorization">reauthorization</Term>** asks PayPal to renew the reservation, which is only useful in an auction that runs for days.

GAVEL's demo creates holds minutes before the capture, so none of this matters there. It matters for a multi-day auction. Because PayPal's real numbers are what is uncertain, GAVEL ships with reauthorization **off** and a real <Term id="sandbox">sandbox</Term> probe, <Term id="gate">gate</Term> <Term id="gate-g3">G3</Term>, running to measure them.

<NotVerified title="PayPal's real numbers are not verified">

The 3 day honor period and the 29 day validity on this page are the assumptions of GAVEL's **<Term id="fake-paypal">fake PayPal</Term>** (assumptions A07 to A11, all marked as modelled, not yet read from the sandbox). Gate G3 is **running**: day 0 was 7 Nov 2026 03:06 UTC and the final reading is about 12 Nov 2026 06:17 UTC. Until it finishes, nothing here claims what PayPal does.

</NotVerified>

## What GAVEL does about it

<Tabs items={['Stale flag', 'Reauthorization', 'Capture refused']}>
<Tab value="Stale flag">

At <Term id="settlement">settlement</Term> start, a hold older than `GAVEL_HOLD_MAX_AGE_MS` (default 2 hours) is flagged `stale` and one `stale_hold` audit row is written. It is a flag only: the hold is still captured. It tells an operator that a hold has aged.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Reauthorization">

Off by default (`GAVEL_REAUTH`, default `0`). When on, before the capture the auction service reauthorizes the candidates still in play whose hold is older than `honorSafeMs`: **2 days 18 hours** (3 days minus 6 hours) unless `GAVEL_REAUTH_HONOR_SAFE_MS` says otherwise. Each hold is reauthorized once, never above its original ceiling, under a request id with a generation number so a retry replays the same request. PayPal is modelled to answer with a **new authorization id**, so the <Term id="journal">journal</Term> stores the new id and keeps the old one in `priorAuthIds`; capture and void then use the new id. The capture waits while a reauthorization is unsettled.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Capture refused">

If PayPal refuses a capture because the authorization is older than its honor period, the <Term id="saga">saga</Term> reads that as a definite refusal that names the hold (422 `AUTHORIZATION_NOT_HONORED_AFTER_HONOR_PERIOD`; the exact name PayPal uses is unverified) and moves to the next candidate, or releases everything if none is left. Nobody is charged wrongly.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## What gate G3 tests

G3 asks: how long does an authorization stay capturable, and does reauthorize extend it? The probe in `packages/payments/gates/g3.ts` runs from the `gate-g3` workflow:

<Steps>
<Step>

### Day 0

A headless browser signs in as the sandbox buyer and approves four orders. One fresh authorization gets an **immediate** reauthorize attempt (its error name is recorded) and is voided. Three others are kept, one per checkpoint, and the creation time is saved in the probe state.

</Step>
<Step>

### Checkpoints at 3.5, 4 and 5 days

Scheduled runs (minute 17 of every sixth hour) pick the saved state up. At each checkpoint that is due, the probe reads the authorization, tries a **1.00 partial capture** (not final), tries a **reauthorize**, and reads again. With day 0 at 7 Nov 03:06 UTC, the checkpoints are due on 10 Nov 15:06, 11 Nov 03:06 and 12 Nov 03:06 UTC.

</Step>
<Step>

### The decision

`decideG3` applies a fixed rule once the three checkpoints are done:

| Observation | Decision | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all three captures succeed | PASS | no reauthorization needed; keep the stale flag |
| a capture fails and a reauthorize works | PASS-WITH-FALLBACK | enable reauthorization |
| a capture fails and no reauthorize works | FAIL | create holds minutes before capture and document it |

</Step>
</Steps>

The state travels between runs as an artifact; the final run writes the GATES.md entry. There is also an early reading of two older G2 authorizations (`gate-g3-early.yml`); its readings are recorded in GATES.md and committed under `docs/evidence/G3-early/`; they are not the G3 result.

## What the simulation shows, and what it does not

<Chip kind="hatched">SIMULATED</Chip> Scenarios S2 to S5 of the 30 day simulation run GAVEL's own code over simulated days against the fake PayPal model, with a fake clock: a capture inside the honor period needs no reauthorization (S2); a capture at day 3.5 works with reauthorization and charges nobody without it (S3); a reauthorization at day 3.04 lets a capture at day 5 succeed (S4); holds never reauthorized expire cleanly and the runner-up is charged its own price (S5). The <Term id="ledger">ledger</Term> balances to the cent in each.

That shows GAVEL behaves correctly **if** PayPal follows the model. It says nothing about whether PayPal does; G3 is the check. See [the simulation](/docs/proof/simulation) and [Gates](/docs/proof/gates).
