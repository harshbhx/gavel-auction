<Held title="What the G3 readings decide">

If the window is shorter than the longest planned auction, auctions are capped to it, or long auctions are left out. If the window covers the planned auctions, the decision becomes GO for card and wallet. Speech stays behind the confirm prompt either way. No G3 result is claimed before the final reading.

</Held>

## Why synthetic data

The speech, simulation and audit-trace datasets use synthetic speech, a simulated clock and scripted auctions on purpose. That is privacy by design: no real person's voice, name or payment detail is collected, stored or published. The sandbox accounts are test accounts with pretend funds.
