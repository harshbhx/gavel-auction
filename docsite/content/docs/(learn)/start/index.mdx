## The five minute path

The path below is the intended flow. A recorded click-through of it on the hosted web app is the next step.

| Step | Persona | What to do | Expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Judge | Open the hosted web URL (see above). | The landing page shows "Start a demo auction". |
| 2 | Judge | Press "Start a demo auction". | A demo room opens with a link for each role. |
| 3 | Auctioneer | Open the console link. | The console shows the lot and its controls. |
| 4 | Bidder | Open a bidder link in a second tab and place a bid. | The bid shows on the big screen. A bid is a hold; nothing is charged. |

## Verify the audit trail yourself

A replay bundle carries a redacted, hash-chained public export of the ledger. `verifyPublicExport` in `@gavel/engine` checks its version, sequence numbers, links and hashes and returns `{ ok: true }` or the first bad index and the reason (`bad_hash`, `bad_prev`, `bad_seq`, `bad_version` or `truncated`). Any edit to an entry makes the result `ok: false` at that index.
