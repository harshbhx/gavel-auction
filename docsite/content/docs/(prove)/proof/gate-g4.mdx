## Question

Does verification against the <Term id="sandbox">sandbox</Term> <Term id="webhook">webhook</Term> id accept real events delivered by PayPal, and reject tampered ones?

## Method

- Workflow: [`gate-g4`](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/blob/main/.github/workflows/gate-g4.yml), by hand, `main` only. A small sandbox flow makes PayPal send real webhooks to a receiver (`tools/g4-receiver`): a <Term id="capture">capture</Term> of 1.00 with `final_capture` and a <Term id="void">void</Term> of a second authorization.
- The log is fetched from the receiver and every delivery goes, as received, to `POST /v1/notifications/verify-webhook-signature` with the raw body spliced in.
- Scripts: `gates/g4.ts` and `gates/g4-events.ts` (event capture alone) in `packages/payments`.
- Needs the repository variable `G4_RECEIVER_URL` and, by name, the secrets `PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID`, `PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET`, `PAYPAL_WEBHOOK_ID`, `SANDBOX_BUYER_EMAIL`, `SANDBOX_BUYER_PASSWORD` and `G4_LOG_TOKEN`.

## Observed

Six real deliveries, each answered HTTP 200 with `verification_status` SUCCESS:

| Event | Count | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CHECKOUT.ORDER.APPROVED | 2 | SUCCESS, SUCCESS |
| PAYMENT.AUTHORIZATION.CREATED | 2 | SUCCESS, SUCCESS |
| PAYMENT.CAPTURE.COMPLETED | 1 | SUCCESS |
| PAYMENT.AUTHORIZATION.VOIDED | 1 | SUCCESS |

Six sanitized fixtures were written to `packages/payments/test/fixtures/webhooks/`. Real events carry fields the fake simulator lacked (`event_version`, `resource_type`, `resource_version`, `summary`, `links`); the fields only the simulator has are `orderId`, `authId`, `captureId`, `amountCents` and `reason`.

## Limits

<NotVerified title="What G4 did not show">

- Tampered-event rejection was not part of this run. It is covered against the fake only.
- The retry schedule and the shapes of `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.DENIED` and `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.PENDING` are not yet recorded.
- Only first deliveries were recorded, so whether a PayPal retry keeps the original `PAYPAL-TRANSMISSION-ID` and time is not yet recorded (assumption W06). Until a run records a retry, the <Term id="verifier">verifier</Term> sends an old transmission time (within seven days) to the verify endpoint instead of refusing it (decision D-0033), and event ids are deduplicated.

</NotVerified>

The owner-run redelivery check that would close W06 is written out in [docs/RUNBOOK-GATES.md](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/blob/main/docs/RUNBOOK-GATES.md). How the server handles webhooks is on the [webhooks page](/docs/safety/webhooks).
