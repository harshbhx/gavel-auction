## Targets

| Target | Value | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hammer to CAPTURED, p95 | 2.5 s | Unverified end to end. The capture call alone has p95 2280.66 ms. |
| hammer to SETTLED, 6 holds, p95 | 6 s | Not shown to hold. The budget assumes each call stays under 1 s at p95, and the void p95 is 1235.96 ms. |
| webhook handler 200, without the verify call | 300 ms | Not measured by this script. |

A settlement of six holds is one truth check, one capture and five voids. With three voids at a time that is two void waves. Using sandbox p95 for the capture and void calls alone gives 2 x 1235.96 + 2280.66 = 4752.58 ms before the truth check, which is not measured yet, so the 6 s target is not shown to hold with margin.

An end-to-end settlement measurement exists (`npm run measure:settlement -w @gavel/e2e`) and runs only through the `measure-sandbox` workflow from `main`. Its status in `docs/GATES.md` at the time of writing is UNVERIFIED, NOT RUN.
