## Targets

| Target | Value | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Term id="hammer">hammer</Term> to CAPTURED, p95 | 2.5 s | Missed. End to end, n=20 with 6 <Term id="hold">holds</Term>: p95 3010.04 ms. The capture call alone has p95 2280.66 ms (n=10, 5 Nov), which leaves little room for the truth check and <Term id="journal">journal</Term> writes. |
| hammer to SETTLED, 6 holds, p95 | 6 s | Met. End to end, n=20 with 6 holds: p95 5591.30 ms. |
| <Term id="webhook">webhook</Term> handler 200, without the verify call | 300 ms | Not measured by this script. |

A <Term id="settlement">settlement</Term> of six holds is one truth check, one capture and five <Term id="void">voids</Term>. With three voids at a time that is two void waves. Using sandbox p95 for the capture and void calls alone gives 2 x 1235.96 + 2280.66 = 4752.58 ms before the truth check. The end-to-end run includes the truth check and came in at 5591.30 ms p95, inside the 6 s target with about 0.4 s of margin on this sample of 20.

The end-to-end settlement measurement (`npm run measure:settlement -w @gavel/e2e`) runs only through the `measure-sandbox` workflow from `main`, in mode `settlement`. Run 42346811713 (20 settlements of 6 holds, 0 failed) has a redacted copy under `docs/evidence/measure/`. Its status in `docs/GATES.md` is PARTIAL: one target met, one missed.
