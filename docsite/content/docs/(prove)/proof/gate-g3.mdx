## Question

How long does a PayPal <Term id="authorization">authorization</Term> stay capturable, and does reauthorize extend it? This decides how long an auction may run between a bid (a <Term id="hold">hold</Term>) and the <Term id="hammer">hammer</Term> (the capture).

## Status

<Charged title="PASS">

G3 passed: the authorization stayed capturable at 3.5, 4 and 5 days and reauthorize was accepted (sandbox, wallet buyer, 1.00 partial capture). This was a real <Term id="sandbox">sandbox</Term> probe. Day 0 was 7 Nov 2026 03:06 UTC; the earlier attempts on 4 to 7 Nov never ran, so no earlier day is claimed. The final checkpoint is [run 43174780092](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/actions/runs/43174780092). Fallback adopted: none.

</Charged>

## Method

- Workflow: [`gate-g3`](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/blob/main/.github/workflows/gate-g3.yml). A manual run on `main` starts the probe (day 0: a headless browser signs in as the sandbox buyer and approves four orders). It also runs on a schedule, every 6 hours, to pick the probe up again and do the checks at each checkpoint. The last run appends the entry to `docs/GATES.md`.
- Scripts: `gates/g3.ts` and `gates/g3-timed.ts` in `packages/payments`.
- Early reading: [`gate-g3-early`](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/blob/main/.github/workflows/gate-g3-early.yml) probes two older G2 authorizations (a 1.00 partial capture and a <Term id="reauthorization">reauthorization</Term> once an authorization reaches its checkpoint age, 3.0 days for the wallet one and 3.5 for the card one). It never touches the G3 probe state. It was an extra early reading, before the final result.
- Secrets by name: `PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID`, `PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET`, `SANDBOX_BUYER_EMAIL`, `SANDBOX_BUYER_PASSWORD`.

## What the nightly run said

The nightly spec R3 (reauthorize after a delay) answered HTTP 422 UNPROCESSABLE_ENTITY and reported `inconclusive`. Reauthorize was therefore unproven on that run; the final G3 probe is the reading that settles it. See the [nightly record](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/blob/main/docs/evidence/nightly/NIGHTLY-42200708552.json).

## Simulated, not real

<NotVerified title="The 30-day run is a simulation">

We simulated 30 days of auctions with a fake clock: holds, <Term id="honor-period">honor-period</Term> expiry, reauthorization, crashes and recovery, with the <Term id="ledger">ledger</Term> balancing to the cent. It runs our code against a model of PayPal's authorization rules (honor period 3 days, validity 29 days, assumptions A07 to A11 in the fake's `ASSUMPTIONS.md`). It says our code behaves correctly if PayPal follows that model, and nothing about whether PayPal does. PayPal's 29-day validity was not verified on the real sandbox. See [the simulation](/docs/proof/simulation) and [SIM-MULTIDAY.md](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/blob/main/docs/evidence/SIM/SIM-MULTIDAY.md).

</NotVerified>

## Limits

- Scope of the pass: sandbox, wallet buyer, a partial capture of 1.00 only. It is not a card order and not a full-amount capture, and the 29-day validity was not probed.
- It uses PayPal-account sandbox orders created by the probe, not a deployed auction.
- The failure modes listed in the simulation file were not seen at 3.5, 4 and 5 days (a capture was never refused). They remain unprobed beyond 5 days: whether a capture after 3 days still works, the exact error name, whether reauthorize inside the honor period is refused, whether it returns a new id, and whether PayPal sends a webhook on expiry.

## What changes the decision

- G3 recorded PASS, fallback none: captures worked at 3.5, 4 and 5 days, so auctions up to that length are inside what was tested. A longer auction, a card order or a full-amount capture would need its own reading.
- The reauthorize path stays available (off by default) if an auction ever needs it.
