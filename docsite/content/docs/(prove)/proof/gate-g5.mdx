## Question

Is <Term id="stt">speech-to-text</Term> accuracy on noisy bid audio high enough to accept bids without a manual confirm?

## Result

<Held title="Tested with synthetic voice clips: within the limit, confirm prompt on every bid">

The first run (before the fixes) read wrong accept 0.083 to 0.167 against a limit of 0.05). Nine of the eleven wrong accepts were a defect in our evaluation, which ignored the <Term id="paddle">paddle</Term> label of the clips. The other errors were traced per clip, the rule parser was fixed for them, and the provider options were tried. Both providers are now at wrong accept 0.000 on both clip sets, but the fixes and the options were chosen after reading the results on the same 24 clips, and the audio is noise-free and synthetic. Spoken bids therefore still go through the <Term id="confirm-prompt">confirm prompt</Term> (assist mode). The payments decision is unchanged.

</Held>

## Held-out noisy set, measured once

On a synthetic held-out set measured once (120 clips: eSpeak speech, simulated room and babble noise at 20, 10 and 5 dB, run 42567832671), the result against the pre-registered thresholds was: Groq wrong accept 0.058 and exact plus recoverable 0.923, Deepgram wrong accept 0.092 and exact plus recoverable 0.744, against 0.05 and 0.90; the in-sample result was 0.000.

| Provider | Exact plus recoverable (line 0.90) | Wrong accept (line 0.05) | Wrong accept at 20, 10 and 5 dB (line 0.05 each) | Confirm rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Groq whisper-large-v3-turbo | 0.923, met | 0.058, missed | 0.050, 0.050, 0.075 (5 dB missed) | 0.175 |
| Deepgram nova-3 | 0.744, missed | 0.092, missed | 0.075, 0.100, 0.100, all missed | 0.233 |

A wrong accept is a clip where the parser answered accept, a bid taken without a confirm prompt, with a pair other than the spoken bid; it is counted over all 120 clips. The parser asked for a confirm on 0.175 (Groq) and 0.233 (Deepgram) of the clips, and on the room microphone no bid is auto-accepted by construction, so these are parser answers and not bids that reached the auction. The audio is synthetic and the noise is simulated. The set was measured once and nothing was tuned on it; any later parser, option or threshold change made after reading these failures counts as in-sample. G5 is not marked passed. Run: [42567832671](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/actions/runs/42567832671); files and sha256 in [`docs/evidence/G5`](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/tree/main/docs/evidence/G5).

## Live socket run

The live /audio socket was run once with both providers (run 42575018866): the 24 committed synthetic clips were streamed in real time with an auctioneer token to the real server process (fake PayPal), the live provider answered 24 of 24 clips for each, and no spoken bid was accepted without a confirm (5 confirm prompts, 19 clips with no room action, 0 accepted). The log scan of the server log found 0 findings with the provider key as the canary. Groq's transcript equals the spoken text for 12 of 24 clips (caption latency after the end of speech p50 191 ms, p95 489 ms); Deepgram's for 21 of 24 (its latency reads 0 ms for every clip, which is not a meaningful number). Synthetic clips and fake PayPal in the server; the web console has an auctioneer microphone (FEAT-62, added after this run; it works only when the server reports a live speech provider, and a bidder phone has no microphone). G5 is not marked passed. Run: [42575018866](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/actions/runs/42575018866); files in [`docs/evidence/voice-live-socket`](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/tree/main/docs/evidence/voice-live-socket).

## Method

- Cached eval: 24 clips (24 utterances) were sent to each provider once and the outputs recorded; the cached eval scores the recorded outputs through the rule parser. It runs in the [`voice-eval`](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/blob/main/.github/workflows/voice-eval.yml) workflow (text and cached modes run on pull requests with no keys; live mode is manual).
- Clip set one: synthetic eSpeak speech, noise-free and robotic (PR #1940).
- Clip set two: the same 24 utterances spoken by one natural text-to-speech voice (PR VOI-01). Still synthetic and noise-free. The current recordings come from the option trials of PR VOI-02 (voice-eval runs 42364412751 and 42365428369).
- Providers: Deepgram nova-3 (`punctuate=true`, `smart_format=false`) and Groq whisper-large-v3-turbo with a bid-words prompt and temperature 0. The first run used Deepgram nova-2 and Groq without a prompt.
- Pass rule for the live run (`npm run gate:g5 -w @gavel/e2e -- --write-gates`, 20 private noisy recordings, `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY`): PASS needs exact plus recoverable confirm at 0.90 or more and wrong accepts at 0.05 or less; a result of 0.70 or more is recorded as tested but not passed; otherwise FAIL.

Before the change (nova-2, no prompt): exact 0.615 to 0.692, wrong accept 0.083 to 0.167. Where the change came from, as exact and wrong-accept clips, Deepgram eSpeak: main 0.615 and 3 clips; the label fix alone 0.769 and 1; plus parser fixes on the old recordings 0.846 and 0; plus provider options 1.000 and 0. The 9 clips that drove a rule went from 0.286 to 1.000 (Deepgram) and 0.857 (Groq) exact; the 15 other clips were 1.000 before and after. With 24 clips one clip moves a rate by 0.042.

System level: nothing is auto-accepted on the room microphone (0 of 24 per run, by construction); with a device microphone and a second reading that always agrees, 0 of 15 auto-accepted bids were wrong. Per-clip causes: `docs/evidence/G5/ERROR-ANALYSIS.md`.

## Parser only: the text-level run

Reference text goes in as recognized text, so this measures the parser and nothing about speech-to-text. On 169 cases in 23 categories (commit 5d2e253, artifact `voice-eval-text`): exact 0.929, wrong accept 0.000, confirm 0.272. The held-out 40 cases were not used to change the lexicon or thresholds. Thresholds stay at AUTO 0.85 and CONFIRM 0.50 (issue 478): the sweep from 0.70 to 0.95 is flat because the parser emits only 0.50, 0.95 and 1.00.

## Limits

- Scope: synthetic speech clips, noise-free in the two 24-clip sets and with simulated noise in the held-out set.
- The 24-clip sample is small, and the parser rules and provider options were chosen after reading the failures on it.
- Latency is not measured by the text mode.
- The nightly harness skipped G5 (20 of 20 private recordings missing), so no live G5 run exists.

## What GAVEL does about it

The confirm prompt protects every voice bid: a spoken bid is never accepted silently, it goes through the confirm gate. See [voice and confirm](/docs/how-it-works/voice-and-confirm). For demos, use the recorded <Term id="replay">replay</Term>, which makes no PayPal call.
