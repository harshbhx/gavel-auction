## Question

Is speech-to-text accuracy on noisy bid audio high enough to accept bids without a manual confirm?

## Result

<Held title="PARTIAL: within the limit on synthetic clips, not proven">

The first run failed (wrong accept 0.083 to 0.167 against a limit of 0.05). Nine of the eleven wrong accepts were a defect in our evaluation, which ignored the paddle label of the clips. The other errors were traced per clip, the rule parser was fixed for them, and the provider options were tried. Both providers are now at wrong accept 0.000 on both clip sets, but the fixes and the options were chosen after reading the results on the same 24 clips, the audio is noise-free and synthetic, and there is no real-room or noisy-room test. Spoken bids therefore still go through the confirm prompt (assist mode). The payments decision is unchanged.

</Held>

## Method

- Cached eval: 24 clips (24 utterances) were sent to each provider once and the outputs recorded; the cached eval scores the recorded outputs through the rule parser. It runs in the [`voice-eval`](https://github.com/harshbhx/gavel-auction/blob/main/.github/workflows/voice-eval.yml) workflow (text and cached modes run on pull requests with no keys; live mode is manual).
- Clip set one: synthetic eSpeak speech, noise-free and robotic (PR #1940).
- Clip set two: the same 24 utterances spoken by one natural text-to-speech voice (PR VOI-01). Still synthetic and noise-free. The current recordings come from the option trials of PR VOI-02 (voice-eval runs 42364412751 and 42365428369).
- Providers: Deepgram nova-3 (`punctuate=true`, `smart_format=false`) and Groq whisper-large-v3-turbo with a bid-words prompt and temperature 0. The first run used Deepgram nova-2 and Groq without a prompt.
- Pass rule for the live run (`npm run gate:g5 -w @gavel/e2e -- --write-gates`, 20 private noisy recordings, `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY`): PASS needs exact plus recoverable confirm at 0.90 or more and wrong accepts at 0.05 or less; PARTIAL needs 0.70 or more; otherwise FAIL.

| eSpeak, noise-free | Deepgram nova-3 | 24 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0.292 | 0.000 |
| eSpeak, noise-free | Groq whisper-large-v3-turbo with a bid prompt | 24 | 0.923 | 0.000 | 0.333 | 0.000 |
| Human-like synthetic voice, noise-free | Deepgram nova-3 | 24 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0.292 | 0.000 |
| Human-like synthetic voice, noise-free | Groq whisper-large-v3-turbo with a bid prompt | 24 | 0.923 | 0.000 | 0.333 | 0.000 |

Before the change (nova-2, no prompt): exact 0.615 to 0.692, wrong accept 0.083 to 0.167. Where the change came from, as exact and wrong-accept clips, Deepgram eSpeak: main 0.615 and 3 clips; the label fix alone 0.769 and 1; plus parser fixes on the old recordings 0.846 and 0; plus provider options 1.000 and 0. The 9 clips that drove a rule went from 0.286 to 1.000 (Deepgram) and 0.857 (Groq) exact; the 15 other clips were 1.000 before and after. With 24 clips one clip moves a rate by 0.042.

System level: nothing is auto-accepted on the room microphone (0 of 24 per run, by construction); with a device microphone and a second reading that always agrees, 0 of 15 auto-accepted bids were wrong. Per-clip causes: `docs/evidence/G5/ERROR-ANALYSIS.md`.

## Parser only: the text-level run

Reference text goes in as recognized text, so this measures the parser and nothing about speech-to-text. On 169 cases in 23 categories (commit 5d2e253, artifact `voice-eval-text`): exact 0.929, wrong accept 0.000, confirm 0.272. The held-out 40 cases were not used to change the lexicon or thresholds. Thresholds stay at AUTO 0.85 and CONFIRM 0.50 (issue 478): the sweep from 0.70 to 0.95 is flat because the parser emits only 0.50, 0.95 and 1.00.

## Limits

- Synthetic audio only, noise-free. No real room, no noisy room, no accents beyond the parser's text cases.
- The 24-clip sample is small, and the parser rules and provider options were chosen after reading the failures on it.
- Latency is not measured by the text mode.
- The nightly harness skipped G5 (20 of 20 private recordings missing), so no live G5 run exists.

## What GAVEL does about it

A spoken bid is never accepted silently: it goes through the confirm gate. See [voice and confirm](/docs/how-it-works/voice-and-confirm). For demos, use the recorded replay, which makes no PayPal call.
