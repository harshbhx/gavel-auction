GAVEL Evidence Explorer

GAVEL is an auction where bidders hold funds with PayPal authorizations and the winner is captured at the hammer. This read-only page charts the project's open datasets. Each section says whether its numbers are real sandbox measurements, a simulation, or synthetic audio. Nothing here is production data and no real money or person is involved.
