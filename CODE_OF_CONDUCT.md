# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a place for focused technical work. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and keep it specific and actionable.
- Accept review comments and disagreement without hostility.
- Respect other people's time, ownership boundaries and decisions recorded in the repository.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, review comments, discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and restrict participation when behaviour breaks this standard. To report a concern, email dhairyapatel0246@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.

Maintainers who are involved in an incident step aside from the decision about it.
